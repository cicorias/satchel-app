**Description**
Satchel saves links and whole web pages as clean, readable Markdown, and keeps them in your own iCloud, not on someone else's server.

SAVE FROM ANYWHERE
• Safari: tap the page menu, then Satchel, to save the link or the whole page.
• Any app: share a link to Satchel from Notes, Messages, Mail, and more. Satchel fills in its title, site, and preview image.
• Add tags and a note as you save, or just tap once.

READABLE, PORTABLE PAGES
• Pages are converted to clean Markdown with headings, lists, code, tables, and footnotes, without the ads and clutter.
• Export any item as a .md file with frontmatter, or copy its Markdown, to use in your favorite Markdown editor.

YOUR LIBRARY
• Search titles, tags, and the full text of saved pages.
• Filter by pages, links, or tag.
• Read saved pages in a clean view; links open in Safari.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Everything lives in your private iCloud database, and it counts toward your iCloud storage.
• No sign-up, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
• Images from websites load only if you choose to.
• Saving works offline, and items sync when you're back online.

## App Review notes

Satchel saves web pages into the user's private iCloud database. There's no account to create, and no demo account is needed.

To review:
1. **Share sheet from Safari:** open any article in Safari, tap Share, then Satchel. If Satchel isn't in the row, tap More and add it. Tap **Save page**, or **Save link**. Open Satchel: the item is in the library. Tap it to read the saved page.
2. **Share from another app:** in Notes, share a link to Satchel and tap **Save link**. In Satchel, the item fills in its title and preview image within a few seconds.
3. **Safari extension:** Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Satchel → turn on **Allow Extension**, and allow All Websites. In Safari, tap the page menu in the address bar → **Satchel** → **Save link** or **Save page**.

Notes:
- **iCloud:** sync uses the device's iCloud account. Without one, saves still work and stay on the device. Settings shows iCloud status.
- **Network use:** link details come from one download of the saved page's HTML; nothing goes to the developer.
- **Onboarding:** it appears on first launch, and again from Settings → How to Save.
- **Export compliance:** `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` is `false`. The app uses only Apple's system encryption (HTTPS, iCloud).
