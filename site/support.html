Support

Turn on the Safari extension (iPhone)

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions.
  2. Tap Satchel and turn it on.
  3. Under permissions, allow it on the websites you want to save from, or choose “Ask”.

Saving from the share sheet

In Safari or any app, tap Share, then Satchel. If it isn't listed, scroll the app row to the end, tap More, and add Satchel.

iCloud

Satchel needs iCloud to be signed in on your device, with iCloud Drive turned on. If iCloud is unavailable, your saves wait on the device and sync when it comes back.
